STEP 4 · PAY YOURSELF FIRST
PAY YOURSELF FIRST. LET AUTOMATION DO THE HARD PART.
PAYDAY
Auto-transfer 10% to savings before you spend a dollar.
EVERY 3 MONTHS
Raise that transfer by 10%. Example: $300 becomes $330.
ALWAYS
Keep it automatic. Willpower runs out; automation doesn’t.
COMMISSION TIP
PAID ON COMMISSION? BUDGET FOR YOUR WORST MONTH.
Build your budget on base pay or your lowest month.
Treat big months and bonuses as savings fuel, not raise-your-lifestyle money.
Set aside taxes first, then invest the rest.
