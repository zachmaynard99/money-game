Most agents grow in one of three ways. One: internet leads, the quote-form leads from comparison sites. SmartFinancial, EverQuote, QuoteWizard, MediaAlpha, All Web Leads. 10

Captive versus independent. This matters on every call. A captive agent sells one carrier's products. Think Farmers, State Farm, Allstate, Farm Bureau. They work under that carrier's brand, systems and rules, and they can't shop a quote to another company. An independent agent is appointed with multiple carriers, so they shop each customer across carriers and have more places to put a hard risk. Most of our customers are Farmers captive agents in Texas and California. So ask early: which carriers do you write with? The answer changes the whole call.
