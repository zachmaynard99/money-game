THE BASICS
WHAT A SYSTEM IS
Inputs go in. Processes turn them into outputs. Feedback tells us what to improve. It all runs inside an environment.
Feed it well. Listen to the feedback.
What Box Digital · Setter training (internal)
2
A system is a set of processes organised to turn inputs into outputs. Feedback is information from the outputs that we use to improve the inputs and processes. The environment is where it all operates. Source: Imperium Labs, Acquisition Genesis (internal training use, paraphrased). Module 7.1, Morgan’s Systems Theory, p1 (graphic from p1).

DOWN TO THE VARIABLES
IT ALL COMES DOWN TO VARIABLES.
System → stimuli → variables. Nothing simply “works” or “fails”. It’s the mix of variables we tried.

10
The client acquisition shell: the macro system holds micro sub-systems, each sub-system holds stimuli, and each stimulus holds variables. What Box Digital · Setter training (internal)
11
Scientific method: formulate the hypothesis, test it, observe the results, iterate. Change one needle-moving variable at a time (ceteris paribus) or the data is unreliable. Sample size example for a cold calling decision-maker pitch: 30 pitches, perfectly tracked. Small samples are streaky, so wait for enough data. Source: Imperium Labs, Acquisition Genesis (internal training use, paraphrased). Module 8.1, p13 to p24; 8.3 Scientific Method Step Summary; 7.1, p18 to p20.
